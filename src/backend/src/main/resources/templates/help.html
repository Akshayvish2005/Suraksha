❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Suraksha Disaster Preparedness System?
Suraksha is a specialized disaster-preparedness education platform designed for educational institutions. It delivers hazard modules (earthquake, flood, fire), knowledge assessments (pre-tests and post-tests), interactive scenario simulations, and scheduled virtual evacuation drills.
How do I unlock an official Certificate of Competency?
To earn a verified certificate for any hazard module (e.g., Earthquake, Flood, Fire), you must complete all prescribed lessons and score 60% or higher on the module's comprehensive Post-Test assessment. Once achieved, your certificate is automatically unlocked in the Certificates tab and can be printed or saved as a PDF.
How do Virtual Evacuation Drills work?
Virtual Drills simulate campus emergency exercises. When a drill window opens, assigned students and teachers join to navigate scenario steps under pressure. Response timings, decision choices, and evacuation routes are recorded to measure institutional readiness.
How is the Readiness Index computed?
The Readiness Index (0–100 score) is formulated as: 40% Training Completion (% of modules completed) + 30% Knowledge Score (average post-test score) + 30% Drill Performance (participation % × decision accuracy). Scores of 80–100 indicate "Well Prepared".
What is the "Drop, Cover, and Hold On" procedure during an earthquake?
DROP down onto your hands and knees. COVER your head and neck (and your entire body if possible) underneath a sturdy table or desk. HOLD ON to your shelter until the shaking stops. If no desk is available, drop down near an interior wall and cover your head with your arms.
