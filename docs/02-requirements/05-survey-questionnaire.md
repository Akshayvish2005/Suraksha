# Requirements-Gathering Questionnaires

Use these short surveys/interviews in Weeks 2–4 to check that the requirements match real
needs. Aim for **at least 20 students, 5 teachers and 2 administrators**. Use a free form tool
(Google Forms, Microsoft Forms) and keep responses anonymous. For students under 18, get the
institution's permission before collecting responses.

Scale for rating questions: 1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree.

---

## A. Student questionnaire (about 5 minutes)

1. Class / year and type of institution (school / college).
2. Have you ever taken part in a fire, earthquake or flood drill? (Never / Once / More than once)
3. If yes, how useful was it? (1–5)
4. I know what to do if an earthquake happens while I am in class. (1–5)
5. I know where the nearest exit and assembly point are. (1–5)
6. I know how to use a fire extinguisher. (Yes / No / Not sure)
7. How would you prefer to learn about safety? (Videos / Games and simulations / Reading / Classroom talk / Practical drills) *— select all that apply*
8. Which device will you mostly use? (Smartphone / Laptop / Computer lab)
9. How good is your internet at school/home? (Very slow / Okay / Good)
10. Preferred language? (English / Hindi / Both / Other: ___)
11. What would make you use a safety app regularly? (open text)

## B. Teacher / faculty questionnaire (about 7 minutes)

1. Subject taught and years of experience.
2. Is disaster safety part of the lessons you teach? (Not at all / Occasionally / Regularly)
3. How often does your institution hold drills? (Never / Yearly / Twice a year / More)
4. What are the biggest problems with physical drills? (Time / Disruption / Low participation / No feedback / Other)
5. Would a virtual drill help? (1–5)
6. What information would you want after a drill? (Participation / Response time / Mistakes / Per-student results / Other)
7. Are you comfortable assigning online modules and reading dashboards? (1–5)
8. What device and internet access do students in your class have?
9. What topics are most important to cover for your region? (Earthquake / Flood / Fire / Heatwave / Cyclone / Other)
10. Any concerns about student data or screen time? (open text)

## C. Administrator / safety officer interview (about 15 minutes)

1. What disaster safety guidelines or plans does the institution follow today?
2. How are they communicated to students and staff?
3. How are drills planned, recorded and reported? Who is responsible?
4. What would you need to see to decide that the institution is "prepared"?
5. What reports would you want from a system like this?
6. Do you have floor plans, evacuation maps and a contact list in digital form?
7. What are the rules or worries about student data and consent?
8. Would you be willing to host a small pilot (about 100 users)? What would you need from us?
9. Who could review the safety content for accuracy?

## How to use the results

| Finding | Where it feeds |
|---|---|
| Preferred learning style, device, internet, language | FR-LM-06, FR-LM-07, NFR-01, NFR-09, NFR-10 |
| Drill problems and reporting needs | FR-VD-05, FR-DR-01 to DR-06 |
| Existing plans and floor-plan availability | FR-EP-01 to EP-03, FR-VD-08 |
| Privacy and consent concerns | FR-UM-09, NFR-06 |
| Pilot willingness | Charter risk R3 and milestone M6 |

Summarise the results (counts, averages, key quotes) in `docs/06-reports/requirements-survey-summary.md`.
